# Shatter

Shatter is a fast space-rock shooter built around a star that bends the action. Turn, thrust, and drift across a field that wraps at every edge, keeping one eye on the rocks and another on the pull of the well.

A wave begins with armored Large rocks. Shoot them until they split, then chase their smaller fragments through the field. Larger rocks take several hits, and each impact leaves a visible mark. Clear every fragment to bring on a faster, denser wave.

The star pulls rocks and bullets, curving shots as they pass. Learn to fire around the bright core and hit a rock on its far side. Rocks swallowed by the core return from a random edge, so the field is never truly clear until the fragments are gone.

A saucer periodically sweeps through to hunt you. The ship's torpedo can turn toward a nearby target in a narrow forward cone, flies straight through the well, and destroys any rock outright. Its ten-second recharge makes every launch count.
